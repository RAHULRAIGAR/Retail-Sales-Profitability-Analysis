# 🛍️ Retail Sales & Business Performance Analysis

> An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze retail sales, customer behavior, product performance, regional trends, sales channels, and order outcomes.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

This project analyzes **2,000 retail orders from 2025** to identify key sales drivers, customer behavior, regional performance, and operational issues.

The goal is to convert raw sales data into **measurable business insights and actionable recommendations**.

> **Note:** The dataset does not contain Cost/COGS data, so actual profit or profit margin is not calculated.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1. 📱 Electronics Drives Sales

**Finding:** Electronics contributes approximately **67.9% of total sales**.

**Why it matters:** Revenue is highly dependent on one category.

**Action:** Protect electronics inventory and pricing while developing other categories.

---

### 2. 🌎 Sales Are Regionally Concentrated

**Finding:** Maharashtra, Telangana and Rajasthan together contribute approximately **36% of total sales**.

**Why it matters:** A large share of revenue comes from a limited number of states.

**Action:** Prioritize high-performing regions while identifying growth opportunities in lower-performing markets.

---

### 3. 🔄 Returns Need Attention

**Finding:** **17.35% of orders were returned**.

**Why it matters:** High returns can increase operational costs and reduce realized sales.

**Action:** Analyze returns by product, category, region and sales channel to identify root causes.

---

### 4. ❌ Cancellations Are Significant

**Finding:** **17.35% of orders were cancelled**.

**Why it matters:** A high cancellation rate indicates potential issues in the customer or order journey.

**Action:** Investigate cancellations by product, payment method, channel and region.

---

### 5. 👥 Returning Customers Are Valuable

**Finding:** Returning customers contribute approximately **47.5% of total sales**.

**Why it matters:** Existing customers are a major revenue source.

**Action:** Strengthen loyalty programs, personalized offers and repeat-purchase campaigns.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

| Area | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| 🔄 Returns | Identify high-return products and improve product/customer experience |
| ❌ Cancellations | Analyze cancellation drivers across channels and payment methods |
| 📱 Electronics | Protect the strongest category while diversifying revenue |
| 👥 Retention | Focus on loyalty and repeat purchases |
| 🌎 Regions | Invest more in high-performing markets and explore new ones |
