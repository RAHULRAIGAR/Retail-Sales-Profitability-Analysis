# 🛍️ Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
An end-to-end data analytics project designed to evaluate retail sales performance, identify profit drivers, and optimize business strategies using SQL and Power BI.

---

## 🚀 STAR Methodology Approach

### 1. Situation
A retail store was experiencing fluctuating profit margins and inconsistent product category sales across different regions, making it difficult for management to identify key growth drivers.

### 2. Task
To analyze historical sales data, uncover underlying profitability issues, and build an interactive dashboard to help stakeholders track key performance indicators (KPIs) in real time.

### 3. Action
* **Data Extraction & Cleaning:** Used **SQL** to query, filter, and aggregate raw sales data, handling missing values and structuring tables for analysis.
* **Calculations:** Implemented complex measures and calculated columns to derive profit margins, discounts, and regional revenue trends.
* **Dashboard Development:** Designed a dynamic **Power BI** dashboard featuring interactive filters, slicers, and visual charts (such as top-performing products and monthly sales trends).

### 4. Result
* Identified that top 20% of products generated nearly 65% of the total profit, helping management streamline inventory.
* Highlighted loss-making sub-categories, enabling the business to adjust pricing strategies and improve overall profit margins.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **SQL:** Data querying, aggregation, and transformation.
* **Power BI:** Data visualization, DAX measures, and dashboard design.
* **Advanced Excel:** Initial data exploration and validation.
